Notice Period and Buyout Policy

A notice-period buyout is not automatic or guaranteed. It may be considered only when the hiring client and the candidate's current employer permit it and the required written approvals have been obtained. Recruiters must not promise a buyout to a candidate before those approvals are confirmed.

The recruiter should record the candidate's stated notice period in TalentDesk and clearly communicate any confirmed release date to the hiring client. A candidate's ability to join early must be verified before the candidate is represented as immediately available.

This sample policy is provided only to demonstrate document upload, retrieval and citations. Replace it with the actual hiring policy before using the application for real recruitment decisions.
